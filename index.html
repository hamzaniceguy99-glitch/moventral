How we work

A coach, not a motivational speaker

Sales content skews toward mindset, hustle and closing lines from 1987. None of it helps when your discovery calls are pleasant and produce nothing.

Moventral is unglamorous: your recordings, a transcript, timestamps where the deal turned, and something specific to try next time. Most sellers find the same two habits are costing them deals across the board.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

What do you sell coaching for — which industries?

Mostly B2B: software, services, agencies and equipment. If we don't know your market, we'll say so; the method transfers, but sector knowledge matters and we won't pretend.

Can I share recorded calls?

Only with the customer's consent and in line with your company's policy and state recording laws. If recording isn't possible, we work from your notes and live role-play instead.

Will this increase my revenue?

Sellers who fix qualification and follow-up usually improve. We don't promise numbers — too much depends on your product, market and pricing.

I'm a founder, not a salesperson.

That's a large share of clients. Founders usually need permission to qualify people out, and a repeatable structure so selling doesn't eat the week.

Do you do team training?

Team coaching covers up to three sellers. For a larger team, write to us with the context.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contract, no minimum term. Access stays open until the end of the month you already paid for.

Where do your deals die?

Twenty minutes on your pipeline and the stage that's leaking most. Free, and specific.
