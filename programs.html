Four programs of 6 to 12 weeks, each built around your real calls and your live pipeline.

Program 01 · Get replies, not volume

Prospecting

Blasting a thousand contacts produces meetings with people who'll never buy. We narrow the list to accounts that fit, write outreach that references something real, and set a daily rhythm you can hold — because consistency beats clever templates.

Program 02 · Qualify, don't pitch

Discovery calls

Most lost deals were unqualified from the start. We build a question set that uncovers the problem, who decides, and what happens if they do nothing — then practice shutting up long enough to hear the answers. Every call ends with an agreed next step or a clean no.

Demos & proposals

The default demo shows everything and lands nothing. We cut it to the three things this buyer told you they need, rehearse the transitions, and rebuild the proposal so the first page contains the decision rather than your company history.
